A table for the whole family

SKP RESTURANTS is located at Karumathampatti, Coimbatore. We cook regional dishes from four southern states and Hyderabadi biryani, and serve them in an air-conditioned hall with wooden décor or on a covered outdoor patio.

Come for a weekday meal, a family lunch or a group dinner. Bigger tables are easy to arrange when you reserve ahead.
